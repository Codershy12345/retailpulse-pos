# 🛒 RetailPulse — POS & Retail Analytics Engine

An end-to-end retail management and analytics solution designed for retail stores. It combines a lightweight, offline-capable Point of Sale (POS) counter with interactive business intelligence and sales dashboards.

---

## 🚀 Live Demo
* **POS Terminal:** [Launch POS Counter](https://Codershy12345.github.io/retailpulse-pos/)

---

## 📌 Project Overview
RetailPulse bridges the gap between daily front-desk billing and high-level retail business analytics:
* **Front Desk:** Fast, offline-first cashier billing terminal with stock tracking.
* **Data Export:** Daily transactions and line items exported directly to standard CSV datasets.
* **Analytics Layer:** Pre-built Power BI and Python models for sales trends, batch expiry tracking, and revenue breakdown.
